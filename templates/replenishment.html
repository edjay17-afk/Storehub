Replenishment plan

Target stock = rounded-up 7 days of average net sales, using the last 30 days for each product and branch. Sales exclude cancelled transactions and subtract returns. Open local transfers reduce shortages; warehouse availability is shared across branches. Suggestions use synced branch balances and local warehouse stock. CSV-imported transfer reports are not deducted from this plan.
